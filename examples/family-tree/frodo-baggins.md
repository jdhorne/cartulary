# Frodo Baggins

## Life

Orphaned young, he was raised by his cousin Bilbo at Bag End and named his
heir. Bearer of the One Ring to Mordor. Like Bilbo before him, he departed
over the Sea in S.R. 1421; his later fate is not recorded in the Shire.
