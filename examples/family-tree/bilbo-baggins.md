# Bilbo Baggins

## Life

The hobbit who went there and back again. Having no children of his own,
he adopted his young cousin Frodo as heir to Bag End. He left the Shire
in S.R. 1421 and passed over the Sea; his fate beyond is not recorded in
the Shire annals.
