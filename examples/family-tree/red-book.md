# The Red Book of Westmarch

## Summary

The memoir begun by Bilbo Baggins and continued by Frodo, recording the
quest of Erebor and the War of the Ring. Its later copies preserved the
genealogical tables of the Shire's leading families, the source of the
dates recorded here.
